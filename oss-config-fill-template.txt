AccessKey ID:

https://image.lingjingxinghe.cn

Notes:
- AccessKey ID and AccessKey Secret must be copied from Aliyun RAM / AccessKey management.
- The app will store them with local secure storage after you click Save.
- I cannot print or export the real Secret in plain text.
- If upload still fails after saving, check whether this RAM user has OSS PutObject permission for bucket ljxhimage2.
